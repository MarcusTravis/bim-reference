Drones for AEC and BIM

The aircraft BIM and survey teams fly for photogrammetry and aerial LiDAR — DJI enterprise platforms first, plus the US-made, VTOL, and fixed-wing options that show up beside them.

Data last checked: Nov 13, 2026 · What changed

Which drones do AEC and BIM teams fly for reality capture?

DJI enterprise platforms lead this chart: the Matrice 350 RTK (with a Zenmuse L2 LiDAR or P1 photogrammetry payload), the compact Mavic 3 Enterprise, and the Matrice 4E. US-made options include the Skydio X10 and Freefly Astro. WingtraRAY (VTOL) and AgEagle eBee X (fixed wing) cover large mapping areas. Popularity here is an editorial judgment.

How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent firm surveys, review directories, or marketplace ranks — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Learning curve is editorial (Gentler / Moderate / Steep) based on typical new-user experience, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other AEC/BIM reality-capture drones on this page.

How cost is rated

  • Cost on this page is a purchase-price band for one unit, or a clearly described kit, not an annual software seat. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.
  • $ — under about $15,000.
  • $$ — about $15,000–40,000.
  • $$$ — about $40,000–80,000.
  • $$$$ — about $80,000+, or quote-only with no public list price found this session.
  • Captions cite an official shop, an authorized reseller listing, or say the band is an estimate. No dollar amounts were invented.

Sources and limits

The Confluence 2025 AEC Tech Stack Survey covers software only and has no product-level drone penetration figures. BuiltWorlds 2026 reported that drones were used by 100% of surveyed organizations that already use reality capture — again a category-level figure, not a brand share. Popularity here is an editorial judgment; no firm-percentage or review counts were invented. Cost is a purchase-price band for one aircraft or a clearly described kit. DJI figures are authorized-reseller listings from Vertex Unmanned and Indy Drone Supply, checked November 2026, because the official DJI store did not return a public US list price to this session. Quote-only aircraft are marked as estimates. Flight time and payload notes come from official vendor pages checked this session.

Frequently asked questions

Which drones on this chart can carry LiDAR?

The DJI Matrice 350 RTK with the Zenmuse L2, and WingtraRAY with its official LiDAR payload option. Freefly Astro can carry a third-party LiDAR payload. The Mavic 3 Enterprise, Matrice 4E, Autel EVO Max 4T, and eBee X do not have onboard LiDAR.

How much does a mapping drone cost?

Authorized reseller listings checked in November 2026 show the DJI Mavic 3 Enterprise at $4,509, the Matrice 4E Worry-Free Plus Combo at $5,189, and the Matrice 350 RTK aircraft at $11,500. A typical M350 + Zenmuse L2 kit is about $27,000. Skydio, Freefly, Autel, Wingtra, and AgEagle aircraft are quote-only here, so their bands are estimates.

Are there US-made drones for AEC work?

Yes. On this chart, the Skydio X10 and Freefly Astro carry the US-made tag.

Why is drone popularity an editorial estimate?

The Confluence 2025 survey covers software only. BuiltWorlds 2026 reported that 100% of surveyed organizations already using reality capture used drones, but that is a category figure, not a brand share. No firm percentages or review counts were made up.
