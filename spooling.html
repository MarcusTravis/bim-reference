Spooling and fabrication

Software that takes a constructible model into the shop: SysQue, Fabrication, EVOLVE, MSUITE, Stratus, and steel platforms.

What is spooling and fabrication software?

Spooling software takes a constructible model into the shop. It splits pipe, duct, and steel into spools and assemblies, then outputs shop drawings, cut lists, and fabrication packages. Some MEP tools run inside Revit (SysQue, EVOLVE, MSUITE, Victaulic Tools). Others are AutoCAD-based or cloud shop-floor platforms (Autodesk Fabrication, Stratus). Steel shops use Advance Steel, SDS/2, and Tekla PowerFab.

How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent firm surveys, review directories, or marketplace ranks — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Learning curve is editorial (Gentler / Moderate / Steep) based on typical new-user experience, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other fabrication tools on this page. Architecture-firm surveys under-count contractor shops, so several cards are honest editorial estimates.

How cost is rated

  • Cost uses a published annual price for one commercial seat when we could find one this session: the cheapest official annual rate, or monthly × 12 if only monthly is listed. Perpetual licenses are rated on the first-year purchase price.
  • Free — no license fee for the core product (open source or free commercial use).
  • $ — under about $500 / year.
  • $$ — about $500–2,000 / year.
  • $$$ — about $2,000–5,000 / year.
  • $$$$ — about $5,000+ / year, or quote-only enterprise pricing with no public list price.
  • If the vendor only quotes, or no public figure turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

Sources and limits

Advance Steel (16 firms) and Trimble SysQue (5) appear in the Confluence 2025 inventory, but that sample is architecture-weighted. G2/Capterra review counts for EVOLVE, MSUITE, and SysQue were 1–2 reviews — too small to rank by, and never invented. Fabrication, EVOLVE, MSUITE, Stratus, Tekla PowerFab, SDS/2, and Victaulic Tools are marked editorial where no survey count was found. Talk to shops that do this work before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

Which MEP spooling tools work inside Revit?

On this chart, Trimble SysQue, EVOLVE Mechanical / Electrical, MSUITE BIM + FAB, and Victaulic Tools for Revit are Revit add-ins. Autodesk Fabrication (CADmep, CAMduct, ESTmep) is the AutoCAD-based suite, and Stratus is a cloud BIM-to-fab platform.

Why are most spooling ratings editorial estimates?

The main survey leans toward architecture firms and undercounts contractor shops. G2 and Capterra had only 1–2 reviews each for EVOLVE, MSUITE, and SysQue, which is too few to rank by. Only Advance Steel (16 firms) and SysQue (5) have survey counts, so the others are marked as editorial estimates.

What software do steel fabricators use?

This chart covers Autodesk Advance Steel and SDS/2 for steel detailing and shop drawings, and Tekla PowerFab for steel fabrication management from estimate to shop floor (paired with Tekla Structures).

How should I choose a spooling tool?

Talk to shops that already do this work before you buy. Most vendors here sell by quote, so cost bands are often estimates. Confirm pricing and content support with the vendor.
